  <p><b>WAC Hapke Photometric Parameter Maps</b></p>
<p> Spatially resolved near-global Hapke photometric parameter maps of the Moon from about 66,000 LROC Wide Angle Camera (WAC) multispectral observations acquired from February 2010 to October 2011. Maps consist of 9 Hapke parameters (w, b, c, B<sub>c0</sub>, h<sub>c</sub>, B<sub>s0</sub>, h<sub>s</sub>, &theta;, &phi;) [1] for each of the 7 bands of the WAC in Equirectangular projection (centered at 0&deg;N, 0&deg;E; 1&deg; by 1&deg; pixel scale). The parameters B<sub>c0</sub>, h<sub>c</sub>, &theta;, and &phi; (band 4, 5, 8, and 9) are constant values as described in [1]. Maps extend 0&deg;E-360&deg;E, 70&deg;N-70&deg;S. These maps were used to normalize the observed reflectance at standard angles incidence=phase=60&deg;, emission=0&deg; in 1&deg; latitude by 1&deg; longitude tiles. For more information on the derivation of the Hapke parameters, please refer to <a href=http://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/2013JE004580/abstract>Sato,
 et al. (2014).</a>

<p><b> PDS Product Naming Convention:</b>  The Hapke photometric parameter maps are archived as 9-band products for each of the 7 WAC bands as well as one 63-band product containing all 9 parameters for all 7 bands of the WAC. The 9 Hapke parameters are archived in the order presented in the table above to create each 9-band product (i.e. Band 1 is for the w parameter and Band 9 has the &phi; parameter). For the 63-band product, the WAC bands are in order of increasing wavelength (i.e. Band 1 contains the w parameter for 321 nm WAC band and Band 63 contains the phi parameter for the 689 nm WAC band).  All the product files are named in the following manner: WAC_HAPKEPARAMMAP_[wavelength, e.g., 321NM or 7BAND]</p>
